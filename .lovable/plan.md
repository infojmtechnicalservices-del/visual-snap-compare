# JM Technical Services website

## What you’ll get
- An eight-page JM Technical Services and Projects website using your navy, orange and white brand direction and the linked site’s bold construction style.
- The existing `/`, `/electrical/`, `/plumbing/` and `/welding/` addresses preserved, plus Building, About, Contact and Quote pages.
- Tap-to-call and WhatsApp actions, a service menu, and easy-to-reach mobile contact buttons.
- Dedicated trade content, FAQs, Cape Town service areas and a filterable gallery.
- A before-and-after renovation comparison like the reference, using clearly identified concept imagery until you provide real project photos.
- Saved before and after screenshots of the website for comparison. The reference-site before screenshot is already captured.

## Enquiries
Contact and quote enquiries will be saved through Lovable Cloud, so they do not disappear. Automatic owner emails require a verified sending domain; call and WhatsApp links work independently.

## Search and hosting
Each page will have its own search title, description and structured data. The site will include a sitemap and search-engine instructions for the supplied JM domain, with static page generation for hosting compatibility.

### Technical details
Use the project’s built-in TanStack routing and page metadata instead of introducing React Router or React Helmet. Preserve trailing-slash public URLs and prerender the public content pages. Verify navigation, the project comparison, mobile layout and an actual saved quote enquiry before completion.

## Content safeguards
Use your exact phone, email, service areas and business name. Do not invent certifications, completed projects, reviews, prices or business history. Generated photos are illustrative, not evidence of JM’s past work.
